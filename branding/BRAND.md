# Verbalix brand

Verbalix combines language transformation with the precision expected from developer tools. The mark is a geometric **V** made from two opposing language ribbons; the central cursor represents the selected text being transformed.

## Assets

- `verbalix-app-icon-master.png`: production 1024×1024, 8-bit RGBA master used to generate platform icons.
- `verbalix-app-icon-generated.png`: original generated raster artwork.
- `verbalix-mark.svg`: scalable standalone mark.
- `verbalix-wordmark.svg`: horizontal lockup for dark backgrounds.

## Typography and voice

- UI: Inter with SF Pro as the native fallback.
- Wordmark: lowercase, semibold, tight tracking.
- Voice: concise, technically precise, calm and helpful.
- Avoid flags, globes and generic chat bubbles; the product is about transforming technical language, not messaging.

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of the center cursor. Do not recolor the two ribbons independently from the approved violet/cyan relationship, stretch the mark or place it on a low-contrast background.
